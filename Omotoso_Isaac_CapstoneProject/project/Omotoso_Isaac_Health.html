Dog Vaccination Schedule

Stay on top of your dog's immunizations with this quick-reference guide. Always consult your veterinarian to tailor a schedule for your dog's specific needs.

Recommended core and non-core vaccinations for dogs by life stage
Vaccine Life Stage Initial Series Booster Type
Distemper / Parvovirus (DA2PP) Puppy 6-8 weeks, then every 3-4 weeks until 16 weeks 1 year later, then every 3 years Core
Rabies Puppy / Adult 12-16 weeks old 1 year later, then every 1-3 years Core
Bordetella (Kennel Cough) Puppy / Adult As early as 8 weeks Every 6-12 months Non-Core
Leptospirosis Puppy / Adult 2 doses, 3-4 weeks apart Annually Non-Core
Lyme Disease Puppy / Adult 2 doses, 2-4 weeks apart Annually Non-Core
Canine Influenza (H3N2 / H3N8) Adult 2 doses, 2-4 weeks apart Annually Non-Core
Note: Core vaccines are recommended for all dogs regardless of lifestyle. Non-core vaccines are recommended based on geographic location, lifestyle, and risk factors. Speak with your vet to determine the best plan for your pet.
